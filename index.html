Dong-Geun Kim 김동근

Ph.D. Candidate, POSTECH Interaction Lab

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Interaction Lab at POSTECH. My research is on vibrotactile perception and tactile communication: how well people can identify and localize vibration patterns on the forearm, and how those patterns can be used to carry information. Right now I am building a tactile Korean system for people with visual or hearing impairments. I have also worked on mid-air ultrasonic haptics and on matching sounds to touch.

Research

  • Tactile communication. Delivering text and other information through vibration patterns on the forearm, so it can be read by touch.
  • Vibrotactile perception. Measuring how the number, placement, and timing of tactors change what people can identify and where they feel it.
  • Mid-air haptics. Making weak ultrasonic mid-air stimuli feel stronger and easier to recognize by adding vibration at the wrist.

Funded projects

  • Tactile Korean System for the Visually and Hearing Impaired (PM) IITP | Jan. 2025 – Present
    Developing a way to deliver Korean text through a wearable vibrotactile array, for people with visual or hearing impairments.
  • Mid-air Haptic Rendering for Digital Twin (PM) IITP | Jan. 2023 – Dec. 2024
    Real-time mid-air haptic rendering for digital twins. Studied how wrist vibration raises the perceived intensity of weak ultrasonic stimuli and improves pattern recognition. Related paper
  • Investigating the Luxuriousness of Haptic Warning Hyundai Motors | Apr. 2023 - Apr. 2024
    User studies on steering-wheel vibration warnings and what makes them feel luxurious.
  • Performance Metric for User Experience Google | Sep. 2021 – Jul. 2023
    Metrics for user experience in digital environments, including sound-to-touch matching for short sounds. Related paper
  • Multimodal Haptic Tool for AR Sports KOCCA | Jun. 2020 – Dec. 2022
    MMGrip, a handheld device that combines vibration, impact, and shear to render contact realistically. Related paper
