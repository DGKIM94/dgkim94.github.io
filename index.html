About Me

Hello, I'm Dong-Geun Kim, a Ph.D. student at POSTECH. My research interests include mid-air haptic rendering, haptic perception, and tactile language communication.

I am actively conducting interdisciplinary research in the field of haptics, leveraging both mechanical and computational knowledge. I also enjoy playing baseball in my free time!
